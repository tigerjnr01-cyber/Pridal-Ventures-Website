About PRIDAL

Construction, development and heavy equipment solutions built around practical results.

WHO WE ARE

Building With Purpose. Delivering With Strength.

PRIDAL VENTURES & CONSTRUCTIONS is a construction and development company based in Kumasi, Ghana.

We provide practical solutions for building construction, road and civil works, land development, construction material supply and heavy equipment services.

Our approach is focused on supporting projects from the ground up — from preparing land and moving materials to construction and site development.

OUR APPROACH

Practical Solutions For Real Projects

We combine construction services, machinery and material supply to support different stages of development.

🏗️

Construction

Supporting residential, commercial and development projects with practical construction solutions.

🚧

Civil Works

Site preparation, land filling, leveling, road works and other civil development services.

🚜

Heavy Equipment

Machinery services for excavation, loading, earthmoving and site development.

🪨

Material Supply

Construction materials including sand, gravel, chippings and related supplies.

OUR VALUES

Strength, Reliability & Commitment

Every construction project requires planning, resources and dependable execution.

We aim to provide clients with straightforward construction and equipment solutions that help projects move forward efficiently.

From land preparation to material supply and construction activities, our goal is to support the work required at every stage of development.
